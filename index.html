01 Flagship project

Assistive robotics · Shared autonomy

Vision-Guided Shared Control for a Full-Arm Prosthesis

A physical human–robot interaction system that combines EMG-driven user input, RGB-D environmental perception, and autonomous motion assistance for coordinated reaching and grasping.

The framework was evaluated on real hardware with able-bodied participants and a transhumeral amputee, comparing vision-guided shared control with continuous muscle-driven control.

02

Robust physiological sensing

Robust HD-sEMG Recovery

Adaptive low-rank matrix completion recovers corrupted or missing high-density sEMG channels by using the spatial structure of dense electrode arrays.

The study addresses mixed-channel corruption and motion interference, with experiments at up to approximately 49% corrupted data. Recovered signals retained approximately 95.5% gesture-recognition performance.

First author IEEE TIM · published

03

Human factors · Physiological interaction

Human Factors and Neuromuscular Coordination in Shared Robotic Control

Beyond task success, this work asks how vision-assisted shared control changes the user’s physiological state and coordination with a robot.

It combines task performance and human-factor assessment with EMG, EEG, and corticomuscular coupling (CMC) to study the user side of intelligent assistance.

Robust EMG–FMG Multimodal Learning

A temporal attention-guided mixture-of-modality-experts approach for hand-gesture recognition when EMG or FMG inputs are noisy or unavailable.
